CurrNow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

CurrNow ("the app", "we", "us") is a simple currency exchange-rate viewer and converter for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data is involved when you use CurrNow and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

CurrNow does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for any personal information. We do not operate our own servers, and we do not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information that you enter into the app. The amounts you type and the currencies you select are processed only on your device and are never sent to us.

2. Exchange-rate data

To display current rates, CurrNow requests exchange-rate data from a third-party provider, ExchangeRate-API (www.exchangerate-api.com). These requests are limited to fetching the latest published rates and do not include any personal information about you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CurrNow shows advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and app usage and interaction data. This information may be used to show personalized or non-personalized advertising.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and by "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

4. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, CurrNow presents a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) that lets you accept or manage how your data is used for advertising. Where it is available, you can change your choices later through the in-app consent options.

5. Children

CurrNow is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention

We do not store your personal data ourselves. Any data processed for advertising is retained by Google in accordance with Google's own policies.

7. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Where required by law, you can withdraw or change your advertising consent through the in-app consent options.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the CurrNow Android application (package com.currnow.app).
